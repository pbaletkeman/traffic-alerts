1) PyTorch Certified Associate (PTCA)
You already work with Python, backend development, and ML‑adjacent tooling. PTCA strengthens:

Tensor operations, training loops, device execution

Model optimization & performance tuning

Data pipelines (Datasets, DataLoaders, Transforms)

This directly supports your GH‑200, Databricks, and backend goals.

2) Kafka Developer (CDAK)
Kafka is easier once you understand how ML systems produce/consume data. CDAK covers:

Producers/consumers, partitions, offsets, compression

Schema evolution (Avro/JSON/Proto)

Kafka Streams & Connect

Exactly‑once semantics, retries, batching, parallelism

This aligns perfectly with your existing Kafka cluster work and your Data Engineer Associate prep.

3) Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA)
CKA is the most operationally heavy:

Cluster architecture, HA control plane, RBAC

Storage, networking, Ingress/Gateway API

Troubleshooting nodes, workloads, autoscaling

Doing this last ensures you already understand the workloads you’ll deploy (ML models + Kafka apps).
